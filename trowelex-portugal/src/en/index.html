05 / FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Before we
begin.

What is a resin-bound surface?+

It is a finish made by mixing aggregate with resin, then applying it over a suitable base. The final specification depends on the intended use and site conditions.

Can it go over the existing surface?+

That depends on the surface's condition and stability, levels and drainage. The base needs to be assessed before the preparation can be specified.

Is it suitable around a pool?+

Trowelex carries out resin-bound pool surrounds. The finish and drainage must be specified for the intended use and existing base.

How long does a project take?+

Timing varies with the area, preparation, weather and curing. Share your project details for an estimate relevant to your space.
