Privacy notice

This notice explains how information you provide when contacting Wilkins Scaffolding Services is used.

Information in an enquiry

The quote form asks for your name, phone number, email address, project location, planned work, property details and preferred date. Photographs are optional. Only send information and images relevant to your scaffolding enquiry.

How your details are used

Your details are provided to Wilkins Scaffolding Services so your enquiry can be reviewed and you can be contacted about your project. The quote form does not sign you up to a marketing list.

Website form processing

When this website is hosted on Netlify with form processing enabled, Netlify processes and stores submitted quote enquiries and uploaded files on behalf of the website operator. Details sent using the email option are handled by your email provider and the business’s email provider. The local preview prepares an email only and does not store or submit enquiries.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not include analytics, advertising trackers or non-essential cookies. It does not save your quote details in browser storage.

Questions about your information

To ask about information you have provided, including a request to access, correct or delete it, contact wilkinsjay05@gmail.com or call +44 7949 914442.
