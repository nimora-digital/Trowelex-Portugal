05 COMMON QUESTIONS

Before
you get started.

Have a question about your project?

Call +44 7949 914442
What information do you need for a quote?+

Send your name, contact details, postcode or project location, a description of the planned work and your preferred date. Property height, access details and photographs will help explain the job.

Do you cover my project location?+

Include your postcode in your enquiry or call Wilkins to confirm whether your project location can be covered.

Can I ask for a particular start date?+

Yes. Add your desired date to the quote form. Availability and the project schedule need to be confirmed directly with Wilkins.

Can I send photographs of the property?+

Yes. Add up to three JPG, PNG or WebP photographs to your quote enquiry, up to 7 MB combined. Only include images that are relevant to the work and that you’re comfortable sharing.

How much will scaffolding cost?+

The quote depends on your project requirements. Send the location, planned work, property details and expected timing, or call to discuss the job.
